## THE FULL PAGE

```
YOUR KEYS. YOUR COINS. TEN NETWORKS, ONE APP.

FBT Swap is a non-custodial cryptocurrency exchange. You connect a wallet you
already own, you swap one token for another through public smart contracts,
and your assets never pass through us. There is no account to open, no email
to hand over, no identity check, and no company wallet holding your money.

If you have ever waited on a withdrawal, watched an exchange freeze accounts,
or discovered that "your" coins were entries in someone else's database, you
already understand why this app is built the way it is. We cannot freeze your
funds. Not because we promise not to — because we never hold them.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SWAP ON TEN NETWORKS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

BNB Smart Chain · Ethereum · Polygon · Arbitrum One · Base · Optimism ·
Avalanche · Linea · Sonic · Solana

Thousands of tokens from public token lists, searchable by ticker, by name or
by contract address. If a token is too new to be listed anywhere, paste its
contract and trade it anyway.

We do not run an order book and we hold no liquidity of our own. For every
swap we ask public aggregators to compare routes across the decentralised
exchanges on your chosen network, then show you the quote, the price impact
and the fee before anything is signed. You sign it. It settles on-chain,
directly between your wallet and the protocol.

Cross-chain moves are handled the same way: a dedicated Bridge screen for
stablecoins, priced and quoted before you commit.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A WALLET, IF YOU WANT ONE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Bring your own — MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Rainbow, Phantom, Solflare, Backpack,
or anything that speaks WalletConnect — or create one inside the app.

The in-app wallet's recovery phrase is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM,
using a key derived from your password through PBKDF2 with 310,000 iterations
(the OWASP-recommended figure). It is never transmitted anywhere, which means
it is not on our servers, and there is nothing on our side for anyone to steal
or subpoena.

  • Fingerprint or face unlock for the app itself
  • TOTP two-factor authentication, compatible with any authenticator app
  • Camera QR scanning for addresses and WalletConnect pairing
  • Encrypted backup and restore that you control
  • A viewer for the NFTs your wallet already holds


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MARKETS, AND WHAT THEY ARE ACTUALLY DOING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Live prices, 24-hour moves, charts and a detail page for every coin.

The Signals screen computes real indicators on your device from public price
data — RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, moving averages, volatility — and combines
them into a single short-term and long-term readout, with the weightings
explained rather than hidden. Chart history answers the questions people
actually ask: how often has this price level held, what was the largest fall
in the period, and is today's volume normal?

Crypto news refreshes daily from public feeds. An AI assistant answers
questions in a threaded conversation, so you can ask "what is gas?" and then
"so why is mine so high?" without starting over.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MORE THAN SWAPPING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  • Auto Orders — limit orders and recurring buy plans. They alert you and
    pre-fill the swap when your price arrives. They do not execute by
    themselves, and the reason is in the next section.
  • Farm — live yields from real protocols, filtered rather than listed
    wholesale, with the risk of each stated on the row.
  • Stocks & Gold — tokenized equities and gold-backed tokens, including PAXG
    from a New York trust-chartered issuer, with the limits of each named.
  • Tap to Pay — exchange an address in person by NFC, then confirm the
    payment in your own wallet. The tap moves the address; the wallet moves
    the money.
  • Points and ranks — earn points for real actions and climb tiers. Your
    points stay on your device and are never published or compared with
    anyone.
  • Buy & Sell and P2P — honest directories of routes that work in your
    region, not dead buttons that fail at checkout.
  • Twelve languages — including Persian, Arabic and Urdu with full
    right-to-left layout. Light and dark themes. Works offline for everything
    that does not need a live price.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT THIS APP WILL NOT DO
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every exchange website promises speed, security and low fees. Here is the part
they leave out.

  • We cannot reverse a transaction. On-chain transfers are final. Send to the
    wrong address and the money is gone — from us, from you, from everyone.
  • We cannot recover a lost recovery phrase. We never had it. If you lose it,
    nobody can help you, and anyone who says otherwise is stealing from you.
  • Auto Orders do not fill by themselves. Automatic execution would mean
    handing a server the power to spend your money. We do not want that power
    and you should not grant it to anyone who asks.
  • We cannot refund a swap you regret, or freeze funds, or unwind a trade.
  • We do not offer leverage or derivatives, and there is no game of chance
    anywhere in this app.

Each of these is the direct cost of you holding your own keys. It is a trade,
and you should make it with your eyes open.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FEES, IN FULL
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Platform fee: 0.70% of the amount you are swapping. It is taken from the input
token inside the same on-chain transaction, and it is shown on screen before
you sign — never after.

Network gas: paid in the network's own coin, to that network's validators.
It never reaches us and we cannot reduce it.

That is the entire list. No withdrawal fee, no deposit fee, no monthly fee, no
spread quietly added to your quote, no charge for using a wallet.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PRIVACY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

No signup. No email or phone number. No advertising SDK, and no third-party
tracker following you between screens. Your settings and your reward points
stay on your device.

One thing we will not pretend about: blockchain activity is public by design.
Every swap — your wallet address and the amounts — is permanently visible
on-chain to anyone who looks. That is true of every non-custodial app,
including this one, and any product telling you otherwise is misinforming you.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RISK
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Crypto assets are volatile and on-chain transactions are irreversible. You can
lose money, including all of it. Nothing in this app is financial advice. The
indicator readouts are arithmetic on past prices — they describe what has
already happened and forecast nothing. Use only what you can afford to lose,
and check the rules that apply where you live.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GET IT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Android APK — direct download, always the latest build:
https://github.com/mshiravi433-ctrl/fbtcryp/releases/latest

Or use it in your browser at fbtswap.ir and install it to your home screen —
it works as an app on Android, iPhone and desktop, with no store required.

Built by Fanous Bazaar Pishgam, Isfahan, Iran.
```
